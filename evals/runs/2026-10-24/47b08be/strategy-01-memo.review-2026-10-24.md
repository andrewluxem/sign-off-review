# Sign-off review: strategy-01-memo.md, profile strategy, stage 3
**Verdict:** Return
**In one line:** The memo gives the annual saving as $1.4M in the Summary and $1.1M in its own table, and the "under six months" payback only works at the higher figure, so the committee cannot approve on these numbers yet.
**Question:** Should Brindlewood close the Boise distribution center and serve its stores from Reno by 30 June 2027? (given)
**Answer the draft gives:** Yes, "We recommend closing Boise and serving its 38 stores from Reno" by 30 June 2027, saving "$1.4M a year from FY2028" for a one-time cost of $600K (this answers the question).
**Assumptions:** I detected profile `strategy` from the content (decision requested, options, costs, savings) and could not confirm it because the user could not be asked. Stage 3 (final) is the default. All five seats ran as parallel subagents and none ran inline. I fetched no benchmark, because the strategy profile only asks that unsourced benchmarks be flagged.

## Missing outside view

- One-time cost ($600K): no past closure, vendor quote or contingency. See finding 8.
- Added line-haul ($85K): no carrier quote or past rate. See finding 8.
- Reno capacity: the only reading is 61 percent in August 2026, with no peak-period utilization or past peak result. See finding 3.
- Reno hiring (14 roles by May 2027): no time-to-fill history for these roles in Reno. See finding 4.
- Two-day delivery: no past result or estimate of the effect on in-stock rates and sales. See finding 2.

## Premortem
1. No one owns the transition ("The team will manage the transition"), so the 14 Reno hires and the March to June 2027 moves slip toward the 31 July 2027 lease end. Boise then has to be held over at the higher rent, which eats into the first year's saving. (internal)
2. The first holiday peak served only from Reno needs more headroom than an August reading of 61 percent implies. With two-day delivery, the Boise-region stores lose in-stock rates and sales worth more than the saving.
3. Reno cannot hire 14 warehouse staff by May 2027, so it cannot take over Boise's volume on schedule.

## What is good and must survive
- The "Decision requested" line states the action and the 30 June 2027 date before any analysis.
- The Summary comes first: the recommendation and the next-day to two-day trade-off appear before any method or numbers.
- "Why now" ties the decision to a dated, sourced trigger: the 31 July 2027 lease end from the lease file and the 2 June 2026 renewal notice, plus the August 2026 utilization report.
- The savings table prices the lease at the $635K renewal rate, nets the 14 Reno roles out of labor, shows the added line-haul as a negative line, and its lines add up to its stated total.
- The rent figures agree across sections: a 22 percent rise to $635K implies about $520K today, which matches "adds $115K a year in rent" in Options considered.
- The one-time cost is split into three named items that add up to $600K, with a March to June 2027 spend window.

## What fixed looks like
One annual saving figure appears in both the Summary and the table, every line of it is sourced, and the payback is recomputed from it with its inputs shown. That clears the Blocker and the tie-out row and moves the verdict to Release with fixes. To reach Release, the memo also needs to:
- size the downside of two-day delivery, with a break-even point and a reversal trigger;
- show Reno's utilization after the move at holiday peak;
- put the decide-by date, renewal deadline, hiring prerequisite and fallback next to the ask;
- name an owner, and make the approval cover the $600K and the 14 roles;
- say how perishable stock will be served;
- list the one-time costs it currently leaves out;
- source or cut the benchmark;
- name and rule out the missing alternatives.
