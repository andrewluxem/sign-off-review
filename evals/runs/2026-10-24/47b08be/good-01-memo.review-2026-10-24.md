**Verdict:** Release with fixes
**In one line:** Every figure ties and the cost holds, but existing visitors arriving later could meet the stop rule, and the memo never rules out the no-cost alternatives, so fix both before the board votes.

## Numbers that do not tie
None.

Every figure stated more than once agrees: $8,411 (Recommendation, cost table, Cost text), 26 Saturdays (2 January and 26 June 2027 are both Saturdays, 26 apart), 13 Saturdays to the end of March (the 13th is 27 March), 53 visitors per hour (212 / 4) and the 26.5 bar, about $4,200 at the stop (13 / 26 x $8,411 = $4,205.50), and the Sunday staff cost ($7,371 x 1.5 = $11,056.50). The cost table sums: 234 x $31.50 = $7,371; $40 x 26 = $1,040; total $8,411.

## Missing outside view
- New versus shifted visitors: the draft has no comparison for how many extended-hours visitors are new, such as Westbrook's total Saturday visits before and after its change. The stop rule rests on this; see finding 1.
- Afternoon demand: the only peer comparison is Westbrook (63 percent within three months, gate counter report, December 2024). It is an internal report, not verified this session; the strategy profile does not require fetching it. Its comparability gaps are finding 4.
- Building cost: $40 per afternoon is a facilities estimate with no past actual cited. Not a decision driver: at double the estimate the total is $9,451, still inside the $12,000 line. No finding.
- Survey intent (46 percent): no conversion base rate is given, but the draft says openly that survey answers overstate visits and builds no forecast on it. No finding.

## Premortem
1. Afternoon counts clear 26.5 per hour because patrons who used to rush in before 14:00 now come later, the trial runs to June, and the board weighs permanent hours at about $16,822 a year with no growth in total Saturday visits. The stop rule was built to measure the wrong thing. (internal)
2. Eastgate cannot fill three long shifts every Saturday within the two-a-month cap (15 shifts in a five-Saturday month need at least 8 people), so afternoons open short-staffed or the cap breaks, and the trial ends early or burns out staff. (internal)
3. January to March demand runs below the September to August average the bar is built on, so the trial stops at the end of March on a seasonal dip before patrons learn the new hours.

## What is good and must survive
- The Decision requested line gives exact hours, dates and the count of Saturdays, and the dates check out.
- The Recommendation's first three sentences give the verdict, cost, budget source, stop point and owner before any method.
- The cost table shows the basis and source of every line and adds up exactly, and the Sunday option is costed from the same base figure.
- The draft names its weakest evidence ("Survey answers overstate real visits") and answers it with a stop rule instead of a forecast.
- The stop rule is a numeric bar worked out in plain arithmetic from the draft's own baseline, and it caps the loss at about $4,200.
