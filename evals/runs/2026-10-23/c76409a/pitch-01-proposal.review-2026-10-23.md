**Verdict:** Return
**In one line:** The pitch sells a 15 percent lift in repeat purchase rate that its own scope (a deck and a workshop) cannot produce or measure, and the figure has no baseline, so Pellucid cannot make a sound hiring decision on this draft.
**Question:** Should Pellucid Pet Foods hire Ostler & Finch for this engagement? (given)

## Missing outside view
- The 15 percent lift in repeat purchase rate, the driver the whole pitch rests on, is compared with nothing: no Pellucid baseline and no result from similar engagements. Covered by finding 2.
- The 212 percent email revenue growth is unverified. It names no client, publication or URL, so no source could be fetched this session, and no figure has been supplied in its place. Covered by findings 7 and 8.

## Premortem
1. Asked how a 15 percent lift is produced and measured when the work ends in a deck and a workshop, and what the 212 percent case was measured from, Ostler & Finch cannot defend either claim in the room and loses credibility with Mara and her finance lead. (internal)
2. Pellucid's data and platform access arrive late, so the three-week audit slips and the November start and handover date move with it.
3. Pellucid's in-house CRM team proposes to design the journeys itself, since the deliverable is a plan the team would have to build anyway.

## What is good and must survive
- The headline is built on a metric the buyer cares about, repeat purchase rate, not on activities. Keep the metric while fixing what supports it.
- The three phases (Weeks 1 to 3, 4 to 8, 9 to 12) add up to the stated 12 weeks and give a frame to attach fees, team time and buyer inputs to.
- The fee is one fixed figure with a two-part milestone split: "$48,000, invoiced half at signing and half at handover."
- The weeks 1 to 3 audit of "the last 12 months of campaign results" is where Pellucid's baseline can come from.
- The named journeys (onboarding, replenishment and winback) fit a repeat-purchase goal.
- The pitch ends on one next step with a date. Keep that form, with a live date and the signing action.

## What fixed looks like
The headline outcome either matches what the 12 weeks deliver, or the scope includes the build, launch and measurement that produce it, with who implements named. The target states Pellucid's baseline repeat purchase rate (or how the audit will set it), whether the lift is relative or in points, the measurement window and whether it is guaranteed. The 212 percent case is named with permission, baselined and backed by a result on the promised metric, or it is replaced. The pitch states exclusions, the team, buyer inputs, handover artifacts tied to the second invoice, a fee split, termination and ownership, and its reply date is still in the future on the day it is sent.
