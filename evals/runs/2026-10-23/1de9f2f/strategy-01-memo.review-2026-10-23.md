# Sign-off review: strategy-01-memo.md, profile strategy, stage 3
**Verdict:** Return
**In one line:** The memo states the annual saving as $1.4M in the Summary and $1.1M in its own table, and the under-six-month payback holds only at the higher figure, so the committee cannot approve on it yet.
**Question:** Should Brindlewood close the Boise distribution center and serve its stores from Reno by 30 June 2027? (given)
**Answer the draft gives:** Yes: "We recommend closing Boise and serving its 38 stores from Reno," saving $1.4M a year from FY2028 for a one-time $600K.
**Assumptions:** The user could not be asked, so profile `strategy` (a memo asking the executive committee for a decision) and stage 3 (a dated memo addressed to its decision makers) were detected from the draft, not confirmed. The draft does not say how many distribution centers the network has; finding 2 uses the two-center case only to show the arithmetic.

- "Why now" ties the timing to dated, sourced evidence: lease end 31 July 2027, the renewal notice of 2 June 2026, the 22 percent rise to $635K and the August 2026 utilization report.
