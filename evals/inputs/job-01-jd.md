# Senior Manager, Lifecycle Marketing

**Fenwick Trail Supply** (outdoor gear retailer, 140 stores plus ecommerce), Denver, Colorado

## What you will do

- Own email, SMS and push programs for 2.4 million loyalty members.
- Build and test lifecycle journeys: welcome, browse and cart abandonment, replenishment, winback.
- Run holdout tests and report incremental revenue to the CMO each quarter.
- Lead a team of four: two CRM specialists, a lifecycle analyst and a copywriter.
- Partner with the loyalty program team on tier and points communications.

## What you bring

- 7+ years in lifecycle or CRM marketing, including 3+ years leading people.
- Hands-on experience with Braze or a similar platform.
- Experience proving incrementality with holdout or control groups.
- SQL for self-serve analysis.
- Loyalty program experience preferred.
