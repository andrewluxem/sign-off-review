# Memo: Close the Boise distribution center and serve its stores from Reno

**To:** Executive committee, Brindlewood Outfitters
**From:** Network planning
**Date:** 14 September 2026
**Decision requested:** Approve closing the Boise distribution center and moving its volume to Reno by 30 June 2027.

## Summary

We recommend closing Boise and serving its 38 stores from Reno. Consolidation saves $1.4M a year from FY2028 against a one-time cost of $600K, so it pays back in under six months. Store delivery in the Boise region moves from next-day to two-day, which store operations has accepted for non-perishable stock.

## Why now

The Boise lease ends on 31 July 2027 (lease file, renewal notice dated 2 June 2026). The renewal terms raise rent 22 percent, to $635K a year. Reno runs at 61 percent of capacity (warehouse system utilization report, August 2026) and can absorb Boise's volume, which was 29 percent of network units in FY2026.

## Annual savings from FY2028

| Line | Annual saving |
|---|---|
| Boise lease avoided, at the renewal rate | $635K |
| Boise labor, net of 14 added Reno roles | $430K |
| Utilities and maintenance | $120K |
| Added line-haul from Reno to Boise-region stores | -$85K |
| **Total** | **$1.1M** |

Industry benchmarks show that consolidating two regional centers typically cuts logistics cost by 20 to 30 percent, so these savings are conservative.

## One-time cost

Severance and retention bonuses: $310K. Moving inventory and racking: $190K. Warehouse system and IT changes: $100K. Total: $600K, spent between March and June 2027.

## Risks

- Two-day delivery could lower in-stock rates in Boise-region stores during the holiday peak. Store operations will monitor.
- Reno needs to fill 14 roles by May 2027.

## Options considered

Renewing the Boise lease keeps next-day delivery but adds $115K a year in rent. Moving Boise to a smaller building was ruled out because no suitable site is available before July 2027.

## Recommendation

Approve the consolidation. The team will manage the transition, with the first inventory moves in March 2027.
