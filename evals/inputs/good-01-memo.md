# Memo: Six-month trial of Saturday afternoon hours at Eastgate Library

**To:** Library board, Marrowby Library District
**From:** Priya Sandoval, Branch manager, Eastgate
**Date:** 5 October 2026
**Decision requested:** Approve opening Eastgate from 10:00 to 17:00 on Saturdays, instead of 10:00 to 14:00, for the 26 Saturdays from 2 January to 26 June 2027.

## Recommendation

Approve the trial. It costs $8,411, fits inside the unspent hours-extension budget, and stops at the end of March if afternoon visits stay low. I will run it and report to the board at the April meeting.

## Why

Saturday is Eastgate's busiest day. The gate counter recorded an average of 212 visitors per Saturday between September 2025 and August 2026, all inside the current four hours (gate counter export, 1 September 2026). In the same export, 41 percent of Saturday visitors arrive in the last hour before closing. In our August 2026 patron survey (380 responses), 46 percent said they would use Saturday afternoon hours.

Survey answers overstate real visits, so the trial carries a stop rule instead of a forecast.

## Cost

| Item | Basis | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Staff time | 3 staff x 3 extra hours x 26 Saturdays = 234 hours at the $31.50 loaded rate (HR rate card, FY2027) | $7,371 |
| Building costs | $40 per afternoon (facilities estimate, September 2026) x 26 | $1,040 |
| **Total** | | **$8,411** |

The FY2027 budget has $12,000 unspent in the hours-extension line (budget book, page 14). The trial uses $8,411 of it.

## Stop rule

The trial continues past March only if afternoon visits average at least half the morning hourly rate. Mornings average 53 visitors per hour (212 over four hours), so the bar is 26.5 visitors per hour. If the trial stops after 13 Saturdays, spending to that point is about $4,200.

## Comparison

Westbrook Library, also in our district, added Saturday afternoon hours in 2024. Within three months its afternoon hourly visits reached 63 percent of its morning rate (Westbrook gate counter report, December 2024). Eastgate's bar of 50 percent sits below that result on purpose.

## Options considered

- Sunday opening. The staff agreement pays 1.5 times the rate on Sundays, so the same hours would cost $11,056.50 in staff time alone.
- Weekday evenings. Eastgate already opens until 19:00 on weekdays, and visits after 18:00 average 18 per hour (gate counter export), the lowest of the week.

## Risks

- Staff fatigue on longer Saturdays. Shifts rotate so no one works more than two long Saturdays a month.
- Low uptake. The stop rule limits the loss to about half the cost.
