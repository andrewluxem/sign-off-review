# Send at 6 a.m. or don't bother

*Lark & Ledger, a weekly brief for retention marketers*

Most email programs leave opens on the table because they send at the wrong hour.

73 percent of marketers say send time is the biggest lever on open rate. Studies show that subscribers decide what to open in the first minutes of their day.

Last year I advised a regional furniture retailer. We moved its weekly promotion from 10 a.m. to 6 a.m., and opens rose 18 percent in the first month. The lesson is simple: the first email in the inbox wins.

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, timing is everything. Your subscribers check email before work, so be there when they do.

**What to do on Monday:** move every campaign to 6 a.m. in each subscriber's local time.
